# FUTURE_DS_02 - Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn and retention patterns for a subscription-based SaaS business using Power BI. The objective is to identify churn trends, understand customer lifetime, evaluate retention drivers, and provide actionable business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze customer churn patterns
* Measure customer retention trends
* Evaluate customer lifetime
* Identify churn reasons
* Recommend strategies to reduce customer loss

---

## 🛠 Tools Used

* Power BI

* DAX

## 📈 Key Insights

* Customer churn rate is approximately 22%.
* Average customer lifetime is around 650 days.
* Customer lifetime varies across different countries.
* Upgraded customers show slightly longer retention.
* Revenue loss differs across churn reasons.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Improve product features to reduce churn.
* Strengthen customer support.
* Encourage subscription upgrades.
* Focus retention efforts on lower-performing regions.
* Monitor churn trends continuously.
